# Staff, attendance and payroll

Open **Staff & payroll** using an owner, manager or accountant account. Cloud shops with the optional admin lock enabled must first unlock administration from the store logo. Employee records hold personnel and pay information; they are separate from the accounts/PINs used to sign in to the POS.

## Employee profiles and attendance

Add a name, phone, job title and agreed monthly salary. Edit the profile when needed; make a former employee inactive instead of deleting their history. Local workspaces can use **POS access accounts** to manage the existing cashier/manager accounts separately.

Choose the working date. **Mark present** and **Check out** record the current time for today. **Edit attendance** records or corrects a selected date, check-in/check-out times, absence or leave. Export the attendance history as CSV. Recorded times do not automatically prorate salary or calculate overtime.

## Advances and employee loans

Choose **Advance / loan** on the employee row. Select Salary advance or Employee loan, then choose whether money is issued or a repayment is received. Enter the actual amount and payment method. Repayments can be made in parts; only that amount is removed from the selected balance.

An advance is recorded as money to recover from a salary. An employee loan has its own balance. Cash issued reduces the open drawer; cash repaid increases it. Card/QR/bank entries record transfers completed outside the POS.

## Monthly salary and payslip

Choose **Pay salary**, confirm the month and agreed base salary, and enter any approved bonus/overtime amount. Enter the advance recovery, loan recovery and other agreed deduction amounts explicitly. The screen previews net pay before confirmation.

Example: Rs. 50,000 gross salary, Rs. 5,000 advance recovery and Rs. 2,000 loan recovery produces Rs. 43,000 net pay. The advance and loan balances are reduced by those recoveries once. A replayed save does not create another payment, and a second salary record for the same employee/month is rejected.

Print a payslip from the salary history, or export payroll and staff-account ledgers as CSV. Review applicable employment, tax and contribution requirements separately: the template does not calculate statutory contributions, tax, holiday rates or automatic overtime rates. Payroll amounts are entered and approved by the shop operator.

## Reports and offline use

Gross salary becomes an operating expense when the salary is recorded. Advances and loan principal are tracked separately so issuing them does not create another salary expense. Income/expense cashflow reports include the actual money issued, received and net salary paid. This avoids counting payroll recoveries as additional cash receipts.

Profiles, attendance, advances, loans, repayments and payroll use the same durable local transaction and optional cloud queue as sales. Prepared offline devices can record them without internet. Reconnect to synchronize; confirm pending records have uploaded before clearing any browser data. If two disconnected devices attempt the same employee/month salary, the conflicting record remains pending for review; do not pay an employee twice from separate offline devices.

Encrypted backups include the personnel and payroll ledgers. Keep those files private. Owner controls hide staff financial information from ordinary cashiers; browser storage still belongs to whoever controls that device.
